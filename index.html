Where are you exploring?

A little data, carefully used.

We count reported listing views, contact-link clicks and visible promotions to help improve local discovery. These are approximate activity counts, not unique visitors or confirmed orders.

Event records contain the business or promotion, event type and time. They do not contain your IP address, a visitor identifier, search terms, referrer, phone number or browsing history. Short-lived daily network fingerprints limit abuse separately from these records. Your cart stays in your browser; an enquiry is recorded only after its separate confirmation.

Your browser privacy signal already turns off optional analytics.

This preference applies to future events and is stored on this browser without a visitor identifier. Disabling analytics does not disable discovery or checkout.
