# Asset credits

These photos are illustrative artwork for clearly fictional sample businesses/categories. They do not depict the listed demo businesses and must not be represented as photographs of actual Rawatbhata merchants. Landscape photos marked Udaipur are not photographs of Rawatbhata. No business endorsement, review, opening status or real location is implied.

## Original share artwork

Phase 6 adds `public/images/share-card.svg` and its 1200 × 630 PNG export for social metadata. The geometric town/landscape composition, typography and Rawatbhata One brand treatment were created for this repository using SVG; no external photograph, proprietary logo or third-party artwork is embedded. `scripts/create-share-card.mjs` reproduces the PNG with the installed browser. Normal builds use the checked-in export and do not need a browser for image generation.
